## Product Purpose

Vector Intelligence is a consultancy for software development, AI, software modernization, and AI agents. The website should explain these services in English and help prospective clients start a conversation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a Next.js application.
- The landing page uses shadcn/ui styled components.
- Implementation should follow DRY, KISS, and SOLID, with particular emphasis on single responsibility and small files.
- The precise primary audience, markets served, service packages, delivery model, and public contact channel remain to be confirmed.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Vector Intelligence.
- Existing logo, square mark, and mascot are in `public/assets/images/` and should be used as supplied.
- Site copy is in English.
- Contact address: `leonardo@vectorintelligence.dev`. Keep it out of static HTML and browser bundles; the contact action is a click-to-email flow, without a form.
